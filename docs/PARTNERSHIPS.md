# Vanta Fiber - Partnerships

## Outreach model

Owner-approved outreach pipeline, not automated spam. No unsolicited mass DMs.

## Lead classes

1. Xbox ASE / fibercraft content creators with measurable engagement.
2. Compatible ARK gaming communities permitting promotion.
3. Independent server-listing communities.
4. Discord bot developers, graphics artists, video editors, community broadcasters.
5. Hosting partners including Nitrado official programme; distinguish direct outreach from formal
   programme eligibility.

## Offers ladder

1. Mutual directory listing and event mention by consent.
2. Guest creator showcase and clip exchange with rights approval.
3. Co-branded free community event or tribe tournament.
4. Sponsored season event with disclosed branding and financial terms.
5. Formal creator affiliate relationship with authorization and deliverable tracking.

## Tracking

Prospect score, follow-up dates, opt-out preferences, conversion from prospect to agreed event.
