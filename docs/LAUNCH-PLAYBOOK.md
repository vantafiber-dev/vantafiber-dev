# Vanta Fiber - Launch Playbook

## Launch sequence

Week -8 through Week -1 and launch: readiness board, funding campaign, hosting quote, closed beta,
public announcement, launch, stabilization.

## Go/no-go anchors

1. Launch offer selected.
2. Demand and willingness to show up verified.
3. Hosting quote and budget accepted.
4. Both target clients joined.
5. Disposable restore and incident drill passed.
6. Launch staff confirmed.
7. Date approved.

## Countdown rules

- A timed launch countdown starts only with a verified date.
- If readiness fails, stop countdown and queued date posts, publish approved delay or status copy.
- Never announce a confirmed launch date before hosting is provisioned and tested.

## Launch day

- Timed global windows, backup staff coverage, status page, first events, bug triage, actual player
  experience measurement.
